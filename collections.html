Our Signatures

Every area is expressed through four signature tops

Named after women who carry grace and strength: Ayla, Laras, Soraya, and Maya. Each silhouette tells a different story of love, roots, and rhythm.

Sleeveless Boxy Top

Ayla Top

Ayla's letter speaks of simplicity, calmness, and quiet warmth. The Ayla top reflects this through its clean minimal front and bright batik side panels, adding soft contrast and an effortless glow without feeling overwhelming.

Finished with a discreet button at the back of the neck for easy wear.

  • 100% Cotton & Linen (plain fabric)
  • Hand-stamped Batik (Batik Cap)
  • Available in 8 colorways
  • Fit: Flexible fit across sizes
Square Neckline Tank

Maya Top

Maya celebrates the beauty of contrast and complexity. Different batik panels at the front and back reflect depth and individuality, while the square neckline adds structure. A piece for women who carry softness and boldness at once.

  • 100% Cotton
  • Hand-stamped Batik (Batik Cap)
  • Available in 9 colorways
  • Fit: Flexible fit across sizes
Sleeveless Kutubaru Top

Soraya Top

Graceful on the outside and simple at heart, Soraya carries a quiet radiance. Its Kutubaru-inspired outer panel brings an elegant heritage touch, while the cropped inner layer keeps it easy and modern. A silhouette designed to shine effortlessly, wrapped in the warmth of tradition.

  • 100% Cotton
  • Hand-stamped Batik (Batik Cap)
  • Available in 6 colorways
  • Available in two sizes: S-M and L-XL
Convertible Strap Top

Laras Top

Inspired by the Javanese word laras, meaning harmony and balance. The wide straps bring structure, while the elasticated bust moves comfortably with you. Calm, balanced, and effortless, Laras is designed to stay in tune with your body and your day.

Designed with a hidden button and three buttonhole options on each strap, making it adjustable and can be worn strapped, crossed, or styled as a tube top.

  • 100% Cotton
  • Hand-stamped Batik (Batik Cap)
  • Available in 8 colorways
  • Adjustable linen strap
The Finishing Touch

Obi Belt

The Obi Belt was created as a finishing touch. A piece that allows every silhouette to feel more personal, playful, and uniquely yours. Featuring two-tone batik combinations and a detachable strap, each piece can be styled and adjusted in multiple ways depending on the mood and moment.

  • 100% Cotton
  • Hand-stamped Batik (Batik Cap)
  • Available in 14 colorways
  • Detachable strap
  • Reversible
