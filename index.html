Our Story

Batik is not meant to be stored away

With Love, From Mom is a casual batik brand designed for everyday comfort. Crafted from curated hand-stamped batik made by artisans across Java and combined with natural fabrics such as cotton and linen, each piece is wrapped with a touch of storytelling.

Because batik is not meant to be stored away — but to be lived in.
