About Us

Who We Are

Batik for Everyday Life

With Love, From Mom is a casual batik brand designed for everyday comfort. Crafted from curated hand-stamped batik made by artisans across Java and combined with natural fabrics such as cotton and linen, each piece is wrapped with a touch of storytelling to create batik that feels light, warm, and effortlessly relaxed without looking overdressed.

Because batik is not meant to be stored away — but to be lived in.

Our Direction

Vision & Purpose

Our Vision

A Natural Part of Everyday Life

To make batik a natural part of modern everyday life. Worn with ease, carried with meaning, and passed forward with love.

Our Purpose

Keeping Batik Alive

To keep batik alive by helping people feel comfortable and confident wearing it in their own way.

“Each piece is designed with a storytelling-led approach, making traditional craftsmanship accessible for your everyday wardrobe.”

— With Love, From Mom
