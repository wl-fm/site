Growing Through Connection

Where Batik Finds Its People

Beyond the garments themselves, With Love, From Mom grows through the people we meet along the way — through markets, conversations, fittings, and small moments shared across different cities. In less than six months, more than 300 handcrafted pieces have found homes across Indonesia and beyond, carried by women from different parts of the world who believe batik deserves to be part of everyday life.

Ubud Taman Baca, Bali • 2026

Ubud Artisan Market: Easter Weekend

A celebration of local culture and mindful craftsmanship set in the heart of Ubud. Being part of the market opened With Love, From Mom to visitors from around the world, many discovering Indonesian batik through our pieces, falling in love with them, and bringing home more than just two.

M Bloc Space, Jakarta • 2026

Pasar Keliling x Matalokal: Pasar Wanodya

Held shortly after Kartini Day, Pasar Wanodya celebrated women, creativity, and modern interpretations of local culture. One of the most memorable moments was seeing women across generations light up as they discovered batik silhouettes they could truly wear every day.

Urban Forest, Jakarta • 2025

Pesta Folka: Gaya Raya

A gathering filled with stylish wastra lovers who already embraced traditional textiles as part of their identity and daily style. It felt especially meaningful to see them connect with With Love, From Mom through playful colors, versatile silhouettes, and a relaxed approach to wearing batik.
